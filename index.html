About Me

Paetyn — PZM Web Design

P

I build custom websites for small businesses across the US — out of Northwest Arkansas.

I started PZM Web Design as a teen because I kept seeing small businesses get burned. They'd pay agencies $3,000-$8,000 for a templated site that didn't actually work — slow, ugly on mobile, no real e-commerce, no SEO. I knew I could deliver better for a fraction of the price.

Now I've shipped real websites for plumbers, kettle corn companies, carpet cleaners, phone repair shops, and more. Every single one is custom-coded — no drag-and-drop, no Wix, no Squarespace. Just clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript that loads fast and looks sharp on every device.

For e-commerce projects, I integrate with Ecwid — that gives clients a real store with secure payments, inventory management, shipping, and tax calculations. The store backend runs on Ecwid's servers; my custom design wraps around it. Best of both worlds.

I keep prices honest because I'm a teen, not an agency. $75 to $400 covers most projects. No hidden fees, no monthly retainer, no upsell game. You pay once and you own the site forever.

Based in the Rogers / Bentonville area in NWA. Working remotely with businesses anywhere in the US.
