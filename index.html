Process

How we'll build your site

From first message to launch, here's exactly what to expect. No surprises, no agency runaround.

01

We Chat

Day 1 · Free, no commitment

You send me a message with your business name, what you do, and what you need the site to do. I respond within 24 hours with a few questions, a custom quote, and a realistic timeline.

  • Honest scope assessment — no inflated quotes
  • Clear timeline based on real scope, not guesses
  • Direct text or email — no sales pitch
02

I Build a Draft

2-7 days · No mockups

Once we're aligned, I get to work. Within a few days I send you a link to your fully working site — not a Figma mockup, the actual coded thing. You can click around, test on your phone, share with friends.

  • Custom-coded HTML, CSS, JavaScript — no templates
  • Mobile-first design — works perfectly on phones
  • Real e-commerce integration if needed (Ecwid)
  • SEO setup so Google can find you
03

We Refine

1-3 days · Unlimited revisions

You tell me what you love and what to change. Colors, copy, layout, photos — anything. I make the changes fast and send back the updated link. Repeat until it feels exactly right.

  • Quick revision turnaround — same day when possible
  • No "change request" fees for in-scope tweaks
  • Direct feedback loop — text me anytime
04

Launch

Day of launch · You own it forever

I help you point your domain at the site (or buy one if you don't have one). I deploy to Vercel — free, fast, reliable hosting. You get the site live, you own everything, no monthly fees, no platform lock-in.

  • Free hosting on Vercel — no recurring cost from me
  • You own the code, the domain, and the data
  • Free post-launch support for the first month

My Approach

How I actually code your site

CUSTOM CODE
No templates, no builders

Every site is hand-coded from scratch. No Wix, no Squarespace, no WordPress. Your site looks how you want, not like everyone else's.

MOBILE-FIRST
Designed for phones first

Most people browse on their phone. I design and test on mobile first, then scale up to tablets and desktops. Every site works perfectly on every screen.

FAST LOADING
Optimized for speed

Pages load in under 2 seconds. Lazy-loaded images, minimal JavaScript, single-file architecture. Google ranks fast sites higher — and customers don't bounce.

REAL SEO
Built to be found on Google

Every site ships with proper meta tags, structured data (Schema.org), Open Graph, sitemap-ready URLs, and geo targeting for local search.

REAL E-COMMERCE
If you need to sell, you sell

I integrate Ecwid for real online stores — secure payments, inventory, shipping, tax. Your store backend handles the complex stuff; my custom design makes it look great.

YOU OWN IT
Full ownership, no lock-in

You get the code, the domain, the data, the deployment. If you ever want to take it to another developer, you can — no platform holding you hostage.

What I Don't Do

Stuff I deliberately avoid

  • Templated platforms (Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy builder) — slow, generic, and lock you in
  • Heavy frameworks for simple sites — React for a 3-page landing page is overkill
  • Monthly retainers for static sites — you shouldn't pay rent for HTML
  • Charging for free stuff — domains and hosting cost what they cost; I don't mark them up
About Me

Paetyn — PZM Web Design

P

I build custom websites for small businesses across the US — out of Northwest Arkansas.

I started PZM Web Design as a teen because I kept seeing small businesses get burned. They'd pay agencies $3,000-$8,000 for a templated site that didn't actually work — slow, ugly on mobile, no real e-commerce, no SEO. I knew I could deliver better for a fraction of the price.

Now I've shipped real websites for plumbers, kettle corn companies, carpet cleaners, phone repair shops, and more. Every single one is custom-coded — no drag-and-drop, no Wix, no Squarespace. Just clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript that loads fast and looks sharp on every device.

For e-commerce projects, I integrate with Ecwid — that gives clients a real store with secure payments, inventory management, shipping, and tax calculations. The store backend runs on Ecwid's servers; my custom design wraps around it. Best of both worlds.

I keep things honest because I'm a teen, not an agency. Every project is quoted individually based on real scope — no inflated agency pricing, no monthly retainer, no upsell game. You pay once and you own the site forever.

Based in the Rogers / Bentonville area in NWA. Working remotely with businesses anywhere in the US.
